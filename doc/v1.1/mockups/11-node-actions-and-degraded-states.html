Node actions, and the console when things go wrong

The two admin dialogs on a node's page, and how the console shows stale data, an unreachable node, a lost connection and a failing Prometheus — never old numbers as current. What's wrong or missing?

Node actions (admin)
node-b leaves the cluster?
node-b hands its 6 pipelines to the others, one at a time, and leaves the ring. The process keeps running, out of the cluster, until it is stopped or restarted.
orders-pg→ node-a
payments-pg, ledger-my→ node-c
… 3 morespread by the ring
ledger-my's running snapshot 7c3a9d5e goes on from its last chunk on node-c. Each hand-off pauses its pipeline for ~10–40 s; lag rises meanwhile.
Ends when every hand-off is done, or after drain_timeout per round (60 s). Progress shows on the node's page and in Events.

Shut down node-b?
node-b leaves the cluster as on the left (hands off its 6 pipelines), then exits. Kubernetes starts it again as cdc-1; it rejoins and the ring rebalances to it after 15 s of stable membership.
With 3 nodes, the other two carry everything until it's back. Without a supervisor, nothing restarts it.
